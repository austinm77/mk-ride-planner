        Unofficial tool, not affiliated with Disney. Live waits and forecasts from the ThemeParks.wiki API, with Powered by Queue-Times.com as a backup. Refreshes every 10 minutes and each time you open the page. When neither answers, typical waits for the time of day are used (from ThemeParks.wiki forecasts for Oct 10–11, 2026; Buzz Lightyear and Big Thunder are estimated from posted waits), adjusted to today's last live reading. Walk times are straight-line distance × 1.4 for path detours. Ride lengths are approximate; TRON includes the locker stop.
